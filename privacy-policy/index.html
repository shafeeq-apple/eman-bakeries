1. Overview

Eman Bakery E-Invoicing is an official enterprise field-sales and ZATCA-compliant invoicing application developed for authorized personnel of IMAN JEDDAH BAKERY.

2. Information Collected and Used

  • Authentication Credentials: Employee Odoo server URL, database name, username, and password are used strictly to authenticate against our company's Odoo ERP server. Credentials are stored encrypted locally on the device.
  • Business & Invoice Records: Customer names, addresses, phone numbers, VAT numbers, product catalogs, and issued tax invoices are synchronized over encrypted HTTPS directly between the mobile app and our company's Odoo 17 ERP server.
  • Local Bluetooth Printing: Bluetooth permissions are used strictly on-device to connect to 80mm thermal receipt printers. Printer hardware identifiers are stored only locally on the device and are never transmitted to external servers.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user or customer data with third parties, advertisers, or analytics networks. The application contains no advertisements.

4. Data Retention and Account Management

Salesman accounts are provisioned and managed internally by IMAN JEDDAH BAKERY administrators. Salesmen or customers may contact developer@emanbakery.online for account access inquiries. Issued tax invoice records are retained in accordance with applicable Saudi Arabian ZATCA and commercial tax regulations.
